# OpenEmbodied

An open knowledge hub for Embodied AI, curating the latest research, foundational knowledge, and essential resources.

## About

OpenEmbodied aims to provide a clear starting point for students, researchers, and developers exploring embodied intelligence.

- **Follow the frontier**: Track recent papers, models, datasets, and open-source projects.
- **Build foundations**: Organize core concepts, classic methods, and introductory materials.
- **Connect research with practice**: Collect tools, tutorials, and reproduction notes.

Beyond collecting resources, we focus on the problems, methods, and limitations behind each contribution.

## Research Areas

- **Vision-Language-Action Models (VLA)**: Multimodal understanding and action generation.
- **Robotic Manipulation**: Grasping, dexterous manipulation, and bimanual coordination.
- **Embodied Navigation**: Environmental perception, spatial understanding, and goal-directed navigation.
- **Locomotion and Whole-Body Control**: Legged robots, humanoids, and motor skills.
- **World Models and Planning**: Environment modeling, action prediction, and long-horizon decision-making.
- **Embodied Learning**: Imitation learning, reinforcement learning, and generalization across tasks.
- **Data and Evaluation**: Robotics datasets, simulation environments, and evaluation benchmarks.

## Curation Principles

- Prioritize original papers, official repositories, and project pages.
- Record publication dates and distinguish preprints from formally published versions.
- Explain why a resource is worth exploring rather than simply listing links.
- Clearly distinguish authors’ findings, personal interpretations, and independently reproduced results.
